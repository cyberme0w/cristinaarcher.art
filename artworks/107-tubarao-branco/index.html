Tubarão-branco

(Great white shark)

Scientific Name: Carcharodon carcharias

It is currently the largest predatory fish. The International Union for Conservation of Nature lists the great white shark as a vulnerable species, and it is included in the Endangered Species List, being protected by several governments.
