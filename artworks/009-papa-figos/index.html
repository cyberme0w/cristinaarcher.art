Papa-figos

(Common golden oriole)

Scientific name: Oriolus oriolus

They grow to about 24 to 25 cm long and have a wingspan of 44 to 47 cm. They can live for ten years or longer and weigh between 65 and 67 grams.

They breed in deciduous forests and agricultural areas with deciduous trees in the vicinity of rivers and lakes, but also in large parks.
