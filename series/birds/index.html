Aves

(Birds)

The series of paintings entitled “Aves” (eng. Birds) was commissioned by the art and culture association CIRCULODEMAJALIS as an illustration of birds from the Montijo region in Portugal. It is part of a traveling exhibition that began at the CIRCULODEMAJALIS headquarters in Canha and is being shown in various cities.
