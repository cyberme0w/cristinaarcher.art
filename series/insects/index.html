Insetos

(Insects)

The “Insects” series began with an initiative by the association Kehrig Summt to raise awareness of the importance of bees. Since then, it has evolved into a collection of all the insects I find in my garden.

These works are available in the Etsy shop as individual prints and postcard collections.
